# The Étienne TV: handover 13-Nov-2026

## Where it stands
- Villa TV home screen, tested on Karoon's 2017 Samsung (UA55MU6103) through TizenBrew. The final TVs will be Sony (Google TV).
- Live at https://ashleydssimcox.github.io/etienne-tv/web/index.html (GitHub Pages). The TV loads `web/index.html` as a TizenBrew module (`ashleydssimcox/etienne-tv@v1`), which pulls the newest files from GitHub Pages every start. Pushing to `main` is enough to update the TV, then Exit and reopen TizenBrew.
- TV autostarts: TizenBrew Autolaunch set to The Étienne, Samsung "Autorun Last App" on, "Autorun Smart Hub" off.

## Done
- Home screen during a stay: large "Welcome," / "The Johns Family", then a smaller gold italic line "to your private residence". Between stays: "Welcome to The Étienne". No time-of-day greeting and no tagline. Surname never takes an apostrophe. Heading scales with surname length: up to 8 letters 110px, 9-12 letters 92px, 13+ letters 76px (the floor); over 16 letters "Family" moves to its own line. Surname comes from Supabase function `get_tv_greeting()` (file in etienne-deploy `supabase/sql/get_tv_greeting.sql`): occupied reservation from 14:00 check-in day to 12:00 check-out day, titles stripped, last word. Checked every 5 minutes.
- Live TV: sidebar categories, channel list, static preview card, genre write-ups. Only Thai PBS plays on the 2017 Samsung; other streams are live (checked from the Mac) but the TV's player fails. Parked.
- Music: five Spotify playlists linked (The Étienne, Morning, Poolside, Sunset, After Dark) plus a gold "Play your own music" card.
- Staff menu: Up x5 or red button, PIN 1042 (remote number keys work). Back on the home screen asks for the PIN; Back inside a page returns home.
- Samsung helper (`service/service.js`, TizenBrew service on port 8085) for opening other apps. Samsung refuses ("Launchpad returns not found"), so Spotify/Netflix don't open from our page on the Samsung. Sony limit doesn't apply. Parked.

- Checked on the TV 13-Nov-2026: changing the guest's surname in ops.theetienne.com shows on the TV after Exit and reopen (or within 5 minutes).
- Leftover: remote branch `welcome-private-residence` could not be deleted from here. Harmless; delete it on GitHub when convenient.
