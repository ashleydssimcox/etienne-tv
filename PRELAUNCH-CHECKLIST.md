# The Étienne TV: pre-launch checklist

Check each item on the first Sony (Google TV) before the villa opens.

## Leaving the app
- [ ] Back inside a page (Music, Streaming, Live TV and so on) returns to the home screen
- [ ] Back on the home screen brings up the staff PIN, and guests can't leave without it
- [ ] Home and Settings on the remote can't leave the app or open TV settings
- [ ] "Exit to the TV menu" in the staff menu works with the PIN

## Apps and music
- [ ] Each Music playlist opens in Spotify on the right playlist
- [ ] Netflix, YouTube, Disney+, Prime Video, Max and Spotify open from Streaming
- [ ] Pressing Home inside an app returns to The Étienne
- [ ] "Reset for next guest" signs guests out of the streaming apps

## Live TV
- [ ] The TV's own tuner channels show and play
- [ ] Check each broadcaster's rules for showing internet streams in a villa
- [ ] Remove the red status box from Live TV

## Mirroring
- [ ] Each TV is named by room (for example "Étienne, Bedroom 1")
- [ ] AirPlay asks for a code on screen
- [ ] iPhone and Android both connect

## Security
- [ ] Move the staff PIN out of the public repository
