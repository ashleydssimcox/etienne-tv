# The Étienne TV: handover 16-Nov-2026

## Where it stands
- Villa TV home screen, tested on Karoon's 2017 Samsung (UA55MU6103) through TizenBrew. The final TVs will be Sony (Google TV).
- Live at https://ashleydssimcox.github.io/etienne-tv/web/index.html (GitHub Pages). The TV loads `web/index.html` as a TizenBrew module, which pulls the newest config.js, platform.js, app.js and styles.css from GitHub Pages every start. Pushing to `main` updates the TV, then Exit and reopen TizenBrew. A new file only reaches the TV if one of those four loads it (photos and logos load by URL from `C.assetBase`).
- Earlier history: `HANDOVER-2026-11-13.md`.

## Done since 13-Nov-2026
- **Dining & Experiences** (built, not yet confirmed on the Samsung):
  - Sidebar of three menus (Mini Bar, The Cellar, Experiences), live from the ops Menu Manager (`menu_items`), refreshed on open and every 2 minutes. Out-of-stock items stay listed, dimmed, "Unavailable".
  - Mini Bar and The Cellar: one column, each row name left, description middle, price right. Long menus: Right enters, Up / Down scroll, Left returns.
  - Experiences: picture cards, Left / Right to browse. Photo is the item's `image_url` if set, otherwise a stock photo matched by name (`experiencePhotos` in config.js), never the same photo twice. Photos in `web/img`, fetched by `.github/workflows/fetch-photos.yml` (free Unsplash licence).
  - QR code top right with a short label (Scan to view / Scan to order / Scan to reserve). Price note at the foot, same wording as the phone menus. No ordering on the TV.
  - One-line subtitles under each menu title.
- **Villa Guide** (built, not yet confirmed): the Guest Guide wording as text, 19 sections in a scrolling sidebar, no QR. Content is a copy in `C.guide` in config.js, taken from `_guideBody` in etienne-deploy index.html. Edits to the guide do not reach the TV until copied across.
- **Streaming** (built, not yet confirmed): single-colour champagne logos on the tiles (Simple Icons, fetched by `.github/workflows/fetch-logos.yml` into `web/img/logos`). Disney+ has no logo in the library, so it shows the name in the serif.
